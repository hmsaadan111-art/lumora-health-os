Executive summary

Lumora is a hospital operating system that links reception, patient records, theatres, beds and a patient app through one automated workflow. For Kingsland, the proposal is a pilot set up before opening, so the hospital starts with one record and one live view of patient flow rather than adding systems later.

For patients

No repeated paperwork

Returning patients who opt in are recognised on arrival, and their history is ready before they reach the desk.

For clinicians

History before the consultation

Visits, allergies, letters and previous prescriptions open on the consultant's screen as the patient checks in.

For operations

A live view of the hospital

Clinic queues, theatre lists and bed status in one place, with patients told when to arrive so waiting areas stay calm.

01

The opportunity

Most hospitals inherit separate systems for reception, records, theatres and outpatient bookings. Staff re-enter the same information, patients repeat their history, and waiting areas fill because nobody can see the live queue.

A new hospital can avoid this. If the workflow is designed before the first patient arrives, every department works from the same record and the same live picture of the hospital.

How it connects

Lumora is designed to sit at the centre of Kingsland's chosen suppliers rather than replace them. Integrations would be confirmed during discovery, using standard interfaces such as HL7 FHIR and supplier APIs where available.

05

Governance and current status

Patient trust comes first. These principles are built into the design, and the details would be agreed with Kingsland's clinical governance team.

Privacy and safety principles

  • Camera recognition is opt-in, and patients can switch it off at any time.
  • Reception confirms identity before any record opens.
  • Cameras cover entrances and reception only.
  • Designed to fit UK GDPR. Data handling agreed before go-live.

Where Lumora is today

Lumora is a concept build with demo data and is not yet running in a hospital. I'm looking for one hospital to shape the first real version with. Kingsland would help decide what is built first, and every workflow would follow Kingsland's own way of working.

06

Common questions

Is Lumora running in any hospital today?

No. Lumora is a working concept with demo data. The pilot would be its first real deployment, which is why Kingsland would have a strong say in what is built first.

What would Kingsland need to provide?

Time with the people who run reception, outpatients, theatres and wards during discovery, details of the suppliers already chosen, and a named lead for decisions during the pilot.

Does camera recognition have to be used?

No. It is opt-in for patients and optional for the hospital. Every other part of the system works without it.

How are commercial terms handled?

Scope and commercial terms would be agreed together after the discovery phase, once both sides know what the pilot will cover.
